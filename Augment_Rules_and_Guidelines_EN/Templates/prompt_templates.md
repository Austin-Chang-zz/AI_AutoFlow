## 💡 **Usage Tips**

### **Template Customization**
1. **Adjust for Projects**: Modify templates to fit specific project needs
2. **Add Specific Information**: Include project-specific constraints and requirements
3. **Keep Updated**: Update template content as projects progress

### **Tips for Better Results**
1. **Specific not Abstract**: Provide specific numbers and requirements
2. **Complete not Fragmented**: Include all relevant context information
3. **Structured Organization**: Use clear headings and lists

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid**
1. **Avoid Vague Descriptions**: Such as "fast", "simple" and other subjective terms
2. **Avoid Insufficient Information**: Provide enough background information
3. **Avoid Requesting Too Much at Once**: Break complex requirements into multiple steps
